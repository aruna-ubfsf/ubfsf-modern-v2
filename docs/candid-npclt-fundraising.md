## Needs
Need: Incarcerated adults + returning citizens often lack consistent, applied training that turns prison education into real nonprofit leadership skills (gov, budgeting, fundraising, comms, program design, reentry planning). Target pop: incarcerated adults + NPCLT alumni. Geo: U.S.—current digital reach in 47 states + DC; near-term scale anchors CA, OK, NY. Current work shows demand + readiness: 16.9K NPCLT starts and 4.2K comps in 3.5 mos (program report), delivered via Edovo; Modules live to ~1.4M tablet users. UBFSF’s 2‑day NPCLT Conference model and in-person prison pilots programs are built to empower incarcerated citizens to lead change. Funding will standardize/refine curriculum, run in‑prison cohort pilots, expand Edovo units, and add fellowships/internships/mentorship so learning becomes measurable reintegration + community impact. Beneficiaries: participants, families, and underserved communities.

## Success criteria
Standout: NPCLT uniquely combines standardized nonprofit-leadership training with a 2‑day in‑prison conference/univeristy course in person cohort model and tablet-based Edovo distribution, delivering both depth (hands-on cohorts) and scale (national digital reach) in one pipeline. It’s already demonstrating demand (16.9K starts/4.2K completions in 3.5 mos; reach in 47 states + DC) and can extend to ~1.4M tablet users while adding fellowships/internships/mentorship to turn learning into leadership outcomes. Success: improved Edovo utilization + completion rates; consistent delivery of refined modules across facilities; pilot/conference attendance and post-session survey gains; large number of participants placed into fellowships/internships/mentorship; alumni engagement and tracked reintegration indicators (employment/education progress, community projects launched).

## Project activity
Edovo Course
UBFSF’s Nonprofit Coaching & Leadership Training (NPCLT) is a tablet-based course delivered via the Edovo platform that empowers incarcerated individuals across America to become effective nonprofit leaders and community change-makers. Created by UBFSF founder Ivan Kilgore from within a maximum-security prison, NPCLT provides comprehensive training across 10 modules: creating a 501(c)(3), organizational management, volunteer recruitment strategy, board development, leadership development, fundraising & development, budgeting & finance, marketing & communications, program development & transition, and resources for successful reintegration. The course addresses gaps in prison education by bridging academic knowledge and real-world application through practical skill-building, advancing UBFSF’s mission of “Rebuilding the Community from within the Community.” Launched Nov 14, 2025, it reached 47 states + DC in 3.5 months, with 16,967 starts and 4,189 completions.

## Details
Fundraising organization: United Black Family Scholarship Foundation

Description
Every day, nonprofit volunteers work with incarcerated individuals to provide self-help, advocacy, and educational programs. While these efforts are invaluable, the question remains: Do we empower incarcerated citizens with the tools and knowledge to become nonprofit leaders themselves? At UBFSF, we believe the answer is clear. Empowering incarcerated citizens to become nonprofit CEOs, board members, and community leaders is essential. No single organization can address the challenges of mass incarceration, police brutality, and systemic inequality alone, but by equipping individuals with the skills to lead, we amplify our collective impact.
